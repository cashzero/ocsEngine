# M7.4 Formation package policy

Formation composition depends on the archetype (references: Beyond the Rhine counter sheets 1B/2B/3B/3F, rules 20.0):

- **Armored** and **mobile infantry** (Axis Panzergrenadier/Führer) divisions are multi-unit: at least two `COMBAT` units, an `ARTILLERY` unit (two at LARGE scale) and reconnaissance. The Allies have no mobile-infantry package; their mobile slot is filled with an armored division.
- **HQs are never formation members.** In the BTR VASSAL module (1.27.0) the 343 PieceSlots that carry the `Formation` mark are all divisional units; none of the HQ PieceSlots carries it, and the HQ counters print their own number (`3`, `18 Abn`, `39 Pz`), not a division. The formation marker only records Formation Method fuel (12.5c A), which the engine keeps as state (`state.fuel.formations`) instead of a marker piece. Each side gets `max(1, ceil(divisionsPerSide / 3))` HQs (role `HQ`) among its independent units (3.2f), named from the module's HQ pieces (`US HQ 3rd Corps`, `Ge HQ 39th Pz Corps`). Each HQ is built from its own PieceSlot, whose faces were read from its front and back images: US corps 14-0 / 9-16 and 18th Abn 8-0 / 6-16; German panzer corps (39, 47, 58 Pz) 10-0 / 7-14 and the other corps 8-0 / 5-10, Gruppe Hohne printing `Grp` instead of `XXX` (range-MA, Combat Mode front / Move Mode back).
- **Infantry** divisions are a single `COMBAT` unit on an XX counter, 4 steps, with no HQ, artillery or reconnaissance (GPID 339 Allied, motorized/truck move face; 826 Axis, leg). Rule 20.0 lets such a division detach Breakdown regiments in play: single-counter infantry and airborne divisions carry a `BREAKDOWN` capability naming their pool's BTR Breakdown counter (US 5-4-3, Ge 3-3-2, Ge LW 4-3-2 for Fallschirmjäger, chosen by 20.0b from the division's AR); Breakdowns are numbered after their division (`1/101.Abn`); see `docs/reports/breakdowns-2026-10-03.md`.
- **Airborne** divisions (its own archetype, never "mobile") are likewise a single leg-mobile XX unit on both sides. The German Fallschirmjäger counter is transcribed from sheets 3F/3B (18-3-3 / 9-3-4). The US airborne divisions are a flavor choice (the sheets print their regiments separately): each XX counter is the sum of its regiments on sheet 2B, one step per regiment (82.Abn 8, 101.Abn 10, 17.Abn 7, 13.Abn 7 on the move face; AR 4, MA 5, leg). Printed values, combat / move face (sheets 2F/2B): 82.Abn 14-4-3 / 8-4-5, 101.Abn 18-4-3 / 10-4-5, 17.Abn 13-4-3 / 7-4-5, 13.Abn 13-4-3 / 7-4-5. Only the GPIDs are placeholders: TODO `airborne-gpid` in `catalog.ts`.

A single-unit division is not a multi-unit formation (3.1c): its counter has no Formation ID, colour band or `F#` affiliation, and it gets no `FORMATION_MEMBERSHIP` relation. Its entry in `sides[].formations` only names the division slot (it counts toward Divisions per side and deploys as its own group).

Generated HQs are not an imported BCS HQ command model. Every unit references the containing side and formation through Foundation Definition IDs. Names and local IDs are deterministic functions of seed, side, formation ordinal, role, and role ordinal.

Point values are attached to reviewed archetypes. Allied and Axis packages use matched costs and the same archetype plan, making the intended balance observable rather than dependent on retry loops.


Naming: formation and unit names use the short counter forms, not full titles or nicknames (`shortFormationName` in `names.ts`): `82.Abn`, `2.A`, `35.Inf`, `12.SS`, `3.PG`, `352.VG`, `6.FJ`, `FBB`; a single-unit division counter is named exactly after its formation, and support units use abbreviations (`Div Arty I`, `Rcn Det`, `Truck Co`, `Pi Bde`).
