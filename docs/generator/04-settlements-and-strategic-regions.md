# M6.4 — Settlements and strategic regions

Settlement candidates exclude water, mountains, and outer-edge Hexes. Suitability combines moisture, low roughness, moderate elevation, and a seed-mixed tie breaker. A minimum Hex distance of three prevents clustering. The best locations become one city, towns, then villages.

First and second entry/supply regions select central land Hexes at opposing horizontal extremes. They are neutral generator metadata and typed Hex features; faction/player ownership is deferred to Scenario composition.
