## How regeneration works

`scripts/ai-match.mjs` generates the initial game, creates one policy per seat, and runs those policies through the gameplay session. Its historical `replay.ok` checks that loading the final save reconstructs its final hash. It does not establish that rerunning a policy produces the historical actions. `scripts/ai-surrogate-transfer-inputs.py` validates saved bytes, endpoints, action identities, and recorded replay results. Its audit explicitly performs no new engine replay.

The new comparison requires policy regeneration from the exact saved initial snapshot. A new generation from seeds can differ when defaults or generation code change. Restore an initial-only session with empty accepted history and correct bindings, configuration, rules RNG state, initial hash, and current hash. Start fresh policies using the saved scenario facts, turn limit, exact weights, and recorded seat seeds. Preserve source save and weight hashes and the current engine and policy build identities. Resolve historical weight paths against the historical artifact root.

`src/ai/seat-runner.ts` keeps plans in per-seat phase memory. The key includes game turn, player-turn index, phase, and segment. `notesOf` in the heuristic stores done units, target assignments, supply claims, truck plans, and other decisions. Accepted-action replay does not reconstruct this memory. A mid-phase restart with empty memory therefore need not reproduce the next historical policy decision. These saved initial snapshots begin at GT1 MOVEMENT, where fresh memory is appropriate.

The policy RNG is recreated for each decision from the seat seed, pre-decision state hash, and decision count at that revision. Rules randomness is separate and comes from the snapshot. Seat swapping exchanges policies while retaining the original even-match seeds, including the seeds belonging to each seat. Do not swap the seat seeds along with the policies.

Compare every accepted action, including actor and action payload, against the saved action at the same index. Require the accepted-action count, final hash, terminal lifecycle, and final VP to match. An action mismatch stops the batch before association scoring. Final hash equality alone cannot demonstrate that the candidate reports arose from the historical policy trajectory. A successful first-game probe establishes feasibility for that game. Every later game still needs its own identity check.

## Feature contract

Use each report's pre-decision gameTurn and state. Include only GT1 through GT3. The observer publishes reports after dispatch in `seat-runner.ts`. Consequently, reading the current session's objective holders inside `onDecision` reads the post-action state. Cache the pre-step state or reconstruct it before the action. Otherwise a successful acquisition could remove its own candidate from the initially-uncontrolled population.

The primary six diagnostics keep path-only objective linkage. A candidate is linked to each distinct configured objective visited after the path origin. One path can link to several objectives. Count each candidate-objective incidence once per group. Filter out an objective when the player already controls it in that report's actual pre-decision state. Keep the full configured objective-point denominator, including objectives with no reports. Divide each player's point-weighted sum by three times that configured total. Counts can exceed one and are not probabilities.

Supply refusals use only `finalSupply.status === 'EVALUATED'` with `preserved === false`. Failed correction probes do not automatically establish final supply refusals. Exhaustion requires at least one retained-score-improving candidate linked to that objective, and every such candidate must have a final supply check that refused it. An untested improving route prevents an exhaustion claim, including routes skipped because another candidate was selected.

The score-blocked window requires at least one linked scored path and no linked path with retained score strictly above its own group's stay score during that player-objective-gameTurn window. A later improving route clears this indicator. A window with no observed path has no observed signal.

For a score crossing, let `m = retainedCandidateScore - stayScore`. Let `t` be the sum of all retained candidate terms with the exact registered term name minus the sum of all stay terms with that name. Count a witness exactly when `m <= 0` and `m - t > 0`. Use exact term names `crowding` and `exposure`. Other terms such as `contact`, `hq-exposed`, `artillery-exposed`, and `rear-exposed` remain intact. Delete the term from both candidate and stay. Sum repeated term names. These witnesses establish arithmetic pressure at the recorded decision, without proving legality or the result of changing the policy.

The budget diagnostic uses `correction.status === 'NOT_EVALUATED'` and `reason === 'CORRECTION_BUDGET'`. `NOT_ELIGIBLE` and an unperformed final supply check mean different things. Preserve actual query truncation, correction eligibility, final-check support, role, assigned target, and coverage so a zero can be distinguished from an unobserved mechanism.

The proposed secondary companion set keeps these same six definitions and the same denominator. It broadens linkage to the union of path entry and progress toward the actual assigned objective. The latter requires `assignedTarget.hexId` to equal that configured objective and the destination's engine hex distance to be strictly smaller than the origin's. Use the engine's actual hex distance. Deduplicate candidates satisfying both bases. Retain `PATH_ENTRY` and `ASSIGNED_OBJECTIVE_APPROACH` flags. Do not call an approach an objective reach, capture opportunity, or legal feasible move. Keep all six companion models separate from the primary six. Freeze this addition before extraction, report every result, and do not search roles, purposes, distance thresholds, or model penalties afterward.

Target assignment and report frequency depend on policy and earlier actions. Both feature sets therefore measure observed policy pressure under the generated trajectory. They are not an invariant census of opportunities and cannot by themselves identify a causal blockage. The companion set can expose an upstream signal that path entry misses, but it remains limited to assigned objectives that the policy actually considered.

## Prediction contract and mathematical checks

Orient each game as variant-player diagnostic minus opponent diagnostic, then average its opposite-seat pair. Use the historical orientation for final VP and early M0, M1, M2, and VP. The target is the seat-averaged variant-versus-baseline VP margin. It is a relative matchup measure. It does not measure the variant's absolute VP gain against an additional baseline-versus-baseline game.

There are four shared seed axes, two in each climate. Each of four splits selects one seed per climate for development and the other for evaluation. Fit on eight development-mean variant records from the other four families. Exclude both half and double members of the held family. Predict each held variant's evaluation-seed mean using that variant's development early features only. Evaluation early features and final outcomes must not affect its prediction.

Freeze ridge precisely. Use training-population standard deviation for each extra diagnostic. Minimize the sum of eight squared residuals plus the square of its standardized coefficient. Leave the intercept and VP coefficient unpenalized. Using mean squared loss instead would change the effective regularization eightfold. Use the same loss convention for diagnostic-alone models. A zero-variance diagnostic receives standardized value zero and coefficient zero. Apply training means and standard deviations to held variants.

A pseudoinverse with an intercept does not generally imply a zero slope for a nonzero constant training predictor. Center VP first and explicitly set its slope to zero when its centered variance is zero if the intended general contract is a training-mean prediction. In this frozen cohort all constant-VP folds have constant zero, so this distinction does not change those predictions. Measured development VP means are zero for every variant in split 2. Several other folds also have constant-zero VP after family exclusion. Report rank and feature variance so readers can see when VP-only is merely the training-mean baseline.

For climate-specific errors, retain the same prediction based on both development climates and replace only the observed evaluation target with that climate's held seed. Training separate climate models would change the registered experiment. State whether the ranking score averages the two evaluation seeds before squaring, as the current protocol specifies. Squaring first and then averaging is a different target because seed noise contributes differently.

Historical early models use the recorded GT4 first-player-turn boundary after WEATHER and FIRST_PLAYER resolve. New movement features exclude every report whose pre-decision gameTurn is 4. Preserve these historical definitions and disclose the slight timing mismatch. This is not evaluation-seed leakage.

Require adversarial controls on the actual scoring path. Changing post-cutoff reports must leave features unchanged. Changing evaluation-seed features or outcomes must leave predictions unchanged, although changed outcomes must change errors. Changing held-family outcomes must leave its fitted coefficients unchanged. Row permutation must leave features, fits, and predictions unchanged within numerical tolerance. Term-deletion examples must include a stay exposure penalty, repeated named terms, exact equality at the boundary, and an untested route that prevents exhaustion.

## Dependence and interpretation

The frozen cohort contains 40 pair records and 80 saved games, but only 65 distinct trajectory identities. Four duplicate-identity groups contain 4, 7, 4, and 4 saved records. Retain all registered variant records. Different weights can produce identical accepted trajectories while producing different candidate scores. Report the duplicates rather than silently deduplicating policy observations.

The four splits produce 40 held-variant predictions, and those predictions repeatedly reuse the same ten variants and four seed axes. Complementary splits exchange development and evaluation roles. Families share scenarios, and identical trajectories cross family boundaries. Neither game count nor prediction-row count is an independent-sample count. Do not attach a decisive population confidence interval, p-value, or qualification claim.

Keep zero effect, historical M0/M1/M2, fitted VP, training mean, and every diagnostic-alone and VP-plus-diagnostic model. Rank paired MSE additions against fitted VP as registered, but preserve errors against zero and the historical models. A winning member of twelve correlated diagnostics is an exploratory lead. A null result must distinguish absent support from a mechanism that occurs but fails to transfer.

The Explain the Number principle shaped this review by requiring trajectory counts, feature support, temporal boundaries, and the exact residual target before interpreting a score. The Type System Discipline principle shaped the separation of final supply refusals, correction-budget omissions, and untested candidates. Their recorded status variants encode different evidence and must remain distinct.
