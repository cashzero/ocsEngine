# Original-policy continuation comparison

Scope remains the existing fifteen WESTERN AXIS_ATTACKS progressObjective30 versus15 pairs. This is a new exploratory continuation experiment. It does not append games to the original thirty-game cohort or change default weights. All initial-state outcomes and first-divergence features have already been seen; new counterfactual outcomes have not.

For every pair use its first divergent accepted action, as fixed by the prior all-pair analysis. Reconstruct the odd match's original axis30/allied15 prefix with real policy calls and runner memory. A separate counterfactual runner calls15 once at that fork, requires the recorded alternate legal action, and returns to original axis30/allied15 immediately thereafter. Keep original rules RNG and seat seeds. The intervention includes action-associated notes. Do not replace a returned action without its corresponding notes.

First run pair1800012 as a pilot. Its control30 continuation must reproduce every original accepted action and its exact final hash. This is one full factual reproduction plus one new counterfactual. Only after this instrument check succeeds, run the fourteen remaining counterfactuals, with short control30 prefix-and-fork checks. Exactly sixteen full continuation histories are scheduled, plus fourteen short factual prefix checks. No new map seeds, dice searches or replacement samples. Every counterfactual must finish, have zero errors/rejections, and independently replay to its final hash. A failed or unsupported case fails full-batch reporting rather than being excluded.

The observed30 factual outcome is taken from the original saved odd match and verified snapshot. For each seed the primary local effect is factual30 axisVPmargin minus single15-intervention axisVPmargin. Also retain axisVP, all-cause Kill and Loss, assigned-target terminal control, and final hashes. The original two-seat policy-pair margin is a separate, previously known result and is not an isolated-action effect.

Before computing these new outcomes, fix three first-divergence features from the prior shared-state scores: added objective-value-scaled progress, added non-progress score cost per added scaled progress, and added exposure score cost per added scaled progress. The latter two are conditional retained-term comparisons, not candidate enumeration. Compare each separately with zero and training-mean baselines using leave-one-seed-out prediction of the local effect. Standardize on the fourteen training rows, use a single-variable ridge with penalty1 and an intercept, and never tune penalties, signs, cutoffs or feature selection on held-out outcomes. Primary error is MAE, secondary MSE; retain all features, zero effects and all fifteen folds. No confidence interval is used to claim generalization from these already examined states and one original dice realization per seed.

This is local-effect exploration across seeds within one weight family and one common later policy. It cannot qualify a predictor of whole-policy improvement or claim independent cross-family validation. The prior pilot with later15 is retained separately to describe continuation dependence at the worst case.
