# progressObjective first-divergence mechanism study

Scope is the existing thirty WESTERN AXIS_ATTACKS games, fifteen swapped-seat pairs, progressObjective30 versus15. No new games and no other weights or metric families.

This analysis is post-outcome exploratory. Include every pair. Select the first unequal accepted action after an identical accepted prefix, independent of pair margin. Verify the same common state by replaying both prefixes, each selected action legal in that state, trace acceptance without fallback, identical unit and assigned objective when supported, and progression score algebra against objective value scaling. Report unsupported pairs explicitly rather than excluding them.

Represent each pair as two actual route choices from a common state, plus recorded score terms and separate outcome timelines. Do not compare scores produced under different weights directly. Affine reweighting of retained terms is a score calculation, not full candidate enumeration or a fresh policy query. The threshold between the two routes is descriptive and conditional on these two routes.

Replay both entire accepted histories, check final hashes, and describe control changes of the assigned target, its terminal margin contribution, and other objectives separately. Two whole-policy trajectories change both seats; they do not isolate the first action. First-divergence local metrics are not independent prospective VP predictors. Preserve zero and negative outcomes, and do not choose a cutoff to fit their signs.

Acceptance requires all15 pairs, all30 final hashes and all25856 accepted actions reconciled, prior worst-pair observation reproduced, and a reviewer checking score/control arithmetic and claim limits. The original research goal remains open. Exact single-action continuation is a separate next unit after memory preservation is established.
